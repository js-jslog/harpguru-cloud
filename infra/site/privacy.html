Privacy

This notice covers the website harpguru.com, including its tuning pages, setup links and embeds. The Harp Guru app collects nothing itself; the App Store and Google Play describe what they collect.

Who is responsible

harpguru.com is run by Joseph Gunn, an individual in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the information below. Contact: contact@harpguru.com.

What is recorded

The site sets no cookies and has no accounts. When you load a page, the hosting service writes a line to a log recording:

  • the date and time;
  • the address you asked for, including the setup after the ? in a link;
  • the country you appear to be in, worked out from your IP address at that moment;
  • your browser's description of itself (its user agent), and the page that linked to us, if your browser sends it;
  • technical details: the response, its size and timing, and the connection's protocol.

Your IP address itself is not kept. Between 9 and 27 November 2026 it was; those records are deleted on the same schedule as the rest, so none remain after the end of February 2027.

Why, and on what basis

To count which pages, setups and tunings are viewed, and from which countries, so we know what to build next; and to keep the site working and secure, by finding errors and misuse. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: understanding how the site is used and running it safely. The records are used only in aggregate. Nothing is used for advertising or to build a profile of you, and nothing is sold.

How long

Each log file is deleted automatically 90 days after it is written. Counts made from the logs, which say nothing about any one visit, may be kept longer.

Who else handles it

Amazon Web Services hosts the site and stores the logs, in its London region, as our processor. Pages are delivered from Amazon's servers nearest to you, which may be outside the UK.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of information about you, or for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted. Because the logs hold no IP address or account, they usually can't be linked to you; if you can tell us when you visited and from what browser, we will look.

You have the right to object to this use of your information. Write to the contact address above and we will consider it against the reasons given here.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
